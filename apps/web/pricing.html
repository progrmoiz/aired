About pricing

Read the docs
Do the people I share with need to pay or sign up?

No. People you send a link to never pay and never need an aired account.

When do Pro, Team and Business open?

They're being built now. Free works today and stays free; publishing and viewing will never need a paid plan.

Will the prices change?

These are the planned prices. If they change before launch, this page changes first.

Can I host aired myself?

Yes. The code is open source under the MIT licence.
