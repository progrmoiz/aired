Questions

About pricing

Anything else is in the docs.

Read the docs
Do the people I share with need to pay or sign up?

No. People you send a link to never pay and never need an aired account.

When do the paid plans open?

Pro is open now. Team and Business are being built. Free works today and stays free; publishing and viewing will never need a paid plan.

Will the prices change?

If prices ever change, this page changes first, and your current subscription keeps its price until you renew.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. After that your pages stay up, the badge comes back, and your own domain points back to aired.sh links.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, a full refund within 14 days of paying. See the refund policy.

Do my agents need Pro?

No. Agents can publish with a free API key. Pro raises the limits and removes the badge.

Can I host aired myself?

Yes. The code is open source under the MIT licence.
