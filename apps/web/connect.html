Connect aired to your AI app

Connect it once, then ask your AI to turn what it made into a link you can send. Free, and it takes about a minute.

ClaudeWeb, desktop and phone
  1. Click Add to Claude. Claude opens with aired filled in.
  2. Check the address reads aired.sh/mcp/account, then click Add.
  3. Sign in to aired and click Allow.
Add to Claude

Claude's free plan allows one added connector. If you have to sign in to Claude first, click the button again afterwards. By hand: Customize → Connectors → + → Add custom connector, and paste the address below.

ChatGPTComing soon

ChatGPT's Free and Plus plans don't let you add your own tools yet, so aired can't be connected there until it's in ChatGPT's plugin directory. Until then, copy the page ChatGPT made and paste it on aired.sh.

Share a ChatGPT page
GeminiPersonal Google accounts in the US
  1. On gemini.google.com, open Settings → Connected Apps.
  2. Under Custom apps, click Add a custom app and paste the address.
  3. Click Next, then sign in to aired and click Allow.

Add it on the website. After that it works in the Gemini phone app too.

PerplexityWeb and Mac
  1. Open Plugins in the sidebar.
  2. Choose Add custom → Add MCP connector → Remote, name it aired and paste the address.
  3. Tick the box, click Add, then sign in to aired.

If you don't see Add MCP connector, your Perplexity plan doesn't include it.

Le ChatMistral
  1. Open Connectors and click + Add Connector.
  2. Switch to Custom MCP Connector, name it aired and paste the address.
  3. Click Connect, then sign in to aired.
Anything elseCopilot and other apps

If your app can't add tools, copy what it made and paste it on aired.sh. You get the same link.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Connecting is free, and so are the pages you publish. Pro raises the limits and removes the aired badge.

Why do I sign in?

So every page your AI publishes lands in your aired dashboard, where you can find, change or delete it later. Signing in takes an email code.

What can aired see?

Only what your AI sends it to publish. aired can't read your chats or your other files.
