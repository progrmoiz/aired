Refund policy

Bad refund policies are infuriating. Here's ours, and it fits on one screen.

Full refund within 14 days

If you ask within 14 days of a payment for aired Pro, you get every cent back. No questions asked.

That covers every payment, renewals included. Forgot your yearly plan renewed? Ask within 14 days of the renewal and we refund the whole year. If someone buys and refunds over and over, we stop selling Pro to them.

Pro costs $108 a year or $12 a month, plus tax where applicable, and the refund includes that tax. Pro is sold to you by Link, Stripe's checkout, so the refund comes back from Link, and your receipt and card statement show Link, not aired.

How to ask

Email support@aired.sh from the address on your Link receipt. If you write from another address, include your GitHub username or the receipt number, so we can find the payment. You can also ask Link support.

A person at aired issues each refund by hand, usually within 2 business days. Your bank then takes about 5 to 10 business days to show the money back.

Please ask us before you open a dispute with your bank. Asking us is faster. A dispute ends Pro the moment it opens, and Pro doesn't come back, however the dispute ends.

What happens after a refund

Refunding your payment for the current period ends Pro right away. Refunding a duplicate charge doesn't. When Pro ends, your pages stay up at the same links, the aired badge comes back, and new publishes follow the Free limits. You can buy Pro again whenever you like.

After 14 days

After 14 days we don't refund, but you can cancel anytime from Manage billing in your dashboard. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for, and we never charge you again.

If we charged you by mistake, like twice for the same period, we refund it whenever you notice. Link may also refund a payment within 60 days in some cases, and any cooling-off right you have under the law where you live still applies.
