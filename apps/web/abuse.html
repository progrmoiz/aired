Trust and safety

Report a page

Anyone can publish to aired.sh. If a page hosts spam, phishing, malware or illegal content, tell us here and we'll review it and take it down.

We remove pages on request after a quick check. We never move a page to someone else's account without its update token.

What happens next

After you send a report

  1. 1We open the pageAnd check it against the terms.
  2. 2We take it downIf it breaks the rules, the link stops working for everyone.
  3. 3We reply if you askedLeave your email and we tell you what we did.

Published it yourself?

Lost the link to manage your own page? Pick "I published this and lost the link to manage it" and we remove it for you.
