## 2. Growth story

### Channels he tried in year one
His list (IH comment, Feb 2021; Starter Story, Nov 2021):
- YouTube videos, including sponsored ones
- Reddit and Slack communities
- Product Hunt and startup directories
- Blog posts
- Lifetime deals
- Linking from tutorials for related products (Gatsby)
- Building in public

He says one or two of these drive 80% of traffic, and for Tiiny that was **SEO**.

### Reddit
- He got his first ~200 users from Reddit and gave 100%-off promo codes as a thank-you.
- Sources: https://www.starterstory.com/stories/how-i-turned-my-teenage-hobby-into-a-profitable-static-web-hosting-service and https://tiiny.host/blog/first-paying-users/

### Product Hunt
- Launched 2020-03-25: 1,500+ visitors and the first sale, via a 50%-off code.
- 2.0 launch Oct 2020 (IndieLessons).
- His later view (2025-04-10): "Product Hunt is dead", useful only for a backlink and short-term traffic: https://x.com/_baretto/status/1910346029775651209

### Lifetime deals and AppSumo
- Ran a $59 "static hosting for life" deal in Sep 2020, with a countdown widget and Facebook LTD groups. It made $1K+ in 3 days.
- Listed on the AppSumo marketplace in Jan 2021 (IndieLessons; AppSumo "also bought with Tiiny Host" page exists: https://appsumo.com/collections/also-bought-with/marketplace-tiiny-host/).
- None of this is current.

### SEO: the core channel
- **Method.** Ahrefs keyword research, then one landing page per job-to-be-done keyword. His bar: KD under ~20 and traffic potential over 1,000 (Indie Bites E80, 2023-03-19: https://indiebites.com/80/transcript). He waited 6+ months to rank. Organic growth began Dec 2020.
- **Keywords he names:**
  - "host zip file" and "html hosting" (IH, Oct 2021)
  - "PDF hosting" (Indie Bites 2023)
  - #1 for "PDF upload" (Starter Story Jan 2024)
  - #1 for upload PDF and top 3 for host HTML (Scaling DevTools, May 2025)
- **SERP check (US, 2026-11-23, SerpAPI):**
  - "host html file": tiiny.host/host-html-file/ ranks #1
  - "pdf upload": tiiny.host/pdf-upload/ ranks #1
  - "host claude artifact": static.app ranks #1; Tiiny's blog post /blog/claude-artifacts/ ranks #6 and pitches the Tiiny MCP
- **Process (Jan 2024).** An SEO analyst picks bottom-of-funnel keywords, a writer publishes 2 posts a week, and a link-builder does outreach. A content agency (Hera Marketing) took DA from 25 to 40+ (Superframeworks).
- **Scale he claims:**
  - 35M Google impressions a year (2025-09-25): https://x.com/_baretto/status/1971228268771922066
  - "35 million Google impressions and 150,000 organic clicks a month with zero advertising spend" (TechRound, 2026-05-15): https://techround.co.uk/interviews/elston-baretto-founder-ceo-tiiny-host/. The two impression numbers look inconsistent; treat them as approximate.
- **Lead product idea.** He saw users converting PDF to HTML in order to upload, so he added direct PDF hosting. It was built in ~2 days, ranked #1, and roughly doubled growth.

### YouTube ("Tiiny Tips")
- Low-production, faceless tutorials that rank in Google's video carousel even where the site doesn't rank, for example "how to share a PDF as a link".
- He says YouTube converts better than SEO but at lower volume (Scaling DevTools 2025).

### Freemium and viral loop
- In 2023 he switched from a 7-day trial to true freemium, and traffic tripled.
- Every free site sits on *.tiiny.site with an "uploaded with Tiiny" banner, which brought thousands of click-throughs a week (Starter Story Jan 2024).
- IndieLessons says freemium took total users from 2,929 to 833,637 over two years.

### Pricing changes
- Added a cheap "Lite" plan (now "Tiny") below the $18 plan. Conversions doubled, and many Lite buyers upgraded straight away (Starter Story 2024).

### Free tools and pSEO page families
- HTML viewer, validator and formatter, PDF viewer, webpage-to-PDF, ChatGPT prompts, a glossary, and others (section 3).
- A Conversation Starters tool (Dec 2024) and a 100 Prompts Library (Mar 2025), per IndieLessons.

### AI wave
- **Uploads.** "Last year 35-40% of uploads were PDFs. This year 80% is code, because ChatGPT and Claude can build the site and still cannot put it online" (God Mode podcast, 2026-09-12: https://castro.fm/episode/VUtUWv).
- **Integrations.**
  - ChatGPT integration (Dec 2024)
  - VS Code plugin with 500 installs (Nov 2024)
  - An MCP server and a Claude Skill (podcast chapters)
  - An "AI creator" at tiiny.host/create, now the homepage banner
  - Use-case pages for Claude artifacts, Lovable, Bolt, v0, Cursor, Codex, Replit, Gemini and Kimi
- **Growth rate.** "20-30% monthly growth during the vibe coding wave" per profitablefounder.xyz (SR-secondary, 2026-04-28).

### Affiliate, referral and creator programs (live, help center updated 2026-11-10)
https://helpdesk.tiiny.host/en/article/tiiny-host-programs-affiliate-referral-creator-r5ah5x/
- **Affiliate.** 20% recurring commission via FirstPromoter, 60-day cookie: https://tiiny.host/affiliates. Launched Dec 2021, and had converted 0 customers by June 2022 (IndieLessons).
- **Referral.** Free users give a friend 50% off and earn 30% of revenue, up to $50 per referral. The homepage loads Cello.so for this.
- **Creator program.** A free account at 5k views, $50 at 10k+ views for short clips on TikTok, Shorts or Reels.

### Paid ads: contradicts the "$0 ads" story
- In 2024-25 he claimed "$0 on ads" (https://x.com/_baretto/status/1932392804472566067), and in May 2026 "zero advertising spend" (TechRound).
- Systemaic's ad-library teardown (2026-07-27) counted:
  - 24 Meta ads, all active, a batch launched around 2026-06-13
  - 36 Google ads, 20 active, the longest running ~292 days
  - 3 LinkedIn ads
  - Source: https://www.systemaic.com/teardowns/tiiny-host
- The live homepage (checked 2026-11-23) loads GTM, the LinkedIn Insight tag (partner 6368202) and an OpenAI ads pixel (`oaiq` from bzrcdn.openai.com).
- **Conclusion:** they now run paid acquisition, at least as tests.

### Other
- **tiiny.com domain.** Bought for $7,000 in Sep 2024 (IndieLessons).
- **Moving upmarket.** In 2025 he discussed enterprise contracts, SSO and security reviews (Indie Board Session, 2025-07-29). LinkedIn copy now pitches client portals and bids and proposals.
- **Community.** Ramen Club, Indie Beers and Indie London. He credits a mentor met through Indie Beers with pointing him to SEO. In 2026 he co-runs hackathons and AI events with Ramen Space.

### His stated lessons (paraphrased)
- Build less, market more. Split time 50/50 between code and marketing, biased toward your weaker side (IH Feb 2021; Starter Story 2021/2024).
- Scatter "marketing seeds", find the channel that grows, then double down on that one before adding others (Starter Story 2024; Messy Growth, 2026-03-02: https://www.messygrowth.io/blog/distribution-beats-product-elston-baretto).
- Start SEO early, because it takes 6-12 months (multiple sources).
- Simplify an existing, validated product for a new audience rather than invent something. He repositioned from developers to non-technical users (Scaling DevTools 2022).
- Ship without features: Tiiny passed $20K MRR without password login or dark mode (X, 2024-09 and 2025-06).
- Build a team instead of starting new projects after $10k MRR; he says that is how he 4x'd (X, 2025-07-29 and 2026-01-15).
- Find a mentor just ahead of you, not a celebrity founder (Starter Story 2024; Scaling DevTools 2025).
- "Distribution beats product", even more so now that AI makes building trivial (Messy Growth 2026).

### Features by tier
From the pricing page comparison table plus the help center:
- **Tiny and up:**
  - Banner removed
  - Alternate .tiiny.co domain
  - QR codes
  - Built-in analytics
  - Feedback mode
  - PHP
  - Version history
  - Hide from search engines
- **Solo and up:**
  - Custom domains
  - Editing
  - Password protection
  - Link tracking
  - Disable PDF download and printing
  - Custom 404 pages
  - Integrations
  - API access
  - AI chat in the editor
- **Pro and up:**
  - Capture emails (verified-email gate)
  - Email allow-list protection
  - **Team members**
  - Priority support
- **Pro Max:** large-file hosting up to 2 TB.

### Team seats
- Available on Pro and above.
- The first extra team member is free. Each seat after that is billed monthly and pro-rata: https://helpdesk.tiiny.host/en/article/how-to-add-team-members-zaqmo3/
- The help article points to the pricing page for seat prices, but the public page does not list them. The site's front-end bundle (main.42ecdc6c.js) has upgrade-modal copy reading "Add up to one more team member" and "Add more team members for +$5 each". So seats are about **+$5 per seat per month**, read from code and not shown publicly.
- Team members can upload and manage every project. Only the owner controls billing and seats.

### Trials, refunds and history
- A 7-day Solo or Pro trial is offered to *some* accounts only. The Pro trial requires a work email, and custom domains are excluded during a trial: https://helpdesk.tiiny.host/en/article/does-tiiny-host-offer-a-free-trial-1co7lv4/
- 7-day money-back guarantee. Subscriptions can be paused for 1-3 months.
- Free links stay up if you log in once every 3 months.
- **2021 prices:** Pro $14/mo and Pro Plus $18/mo (Starter Story 2021).
