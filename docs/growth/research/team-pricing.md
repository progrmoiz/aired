# Individual, team and business pricing: comparables for aired (read 2026-11-23)

All prices USD, read from the live pricing pages on 2026-11-23 unless marked otherwise. "Annual" means the per-month price when billed yearly. "n/r" means the page loaded but the number was not readable. Method: `wit read` (Jina) first; where prices were rendered client-side (ChatGPT, Tiiny, Netlify, Vercel, Notion, Gamma, Webflow, Framer, Figma, Loom, Pitch) the page was opened in a browser and the toggles clicked. DocSend refused automated fetching, so it is from recent third-party pages (marked).

aired proposal being tested: Free; Pro $9/mo (custom domain, no badge, analytics, API key, named-people sharing); Team $10/editor/mo, min 3, viewers and guests free (workspace, roles, folders, comments); Business $20/editor/mo, min 5 (SSO, sharing policies, audit log).

### Claude artifact sharing limits (support.claude.com "Share artifacts", read 2026-11-23)
- "Anyone with the link": Pro, Max, Ultra, Team, Enterprise. **Not Free.**
- Share within the organization: Team, Enterprise. Share with groups: Enterprise only.
- Invite specific people by email (beta): every paid plan; each invitee signs in with a Claude account; **up to 50 outside people per artifact**.
- Team and Enterprise: artifacts stay inside the org by default; an owner decides whether users can share outside it, and turning off external sharing breaks existing public links.
- Artifacts that connect to other websites (or contain an unchecked upload) cannot use "Anyone with the link".
- Link viewers use their own access: Claude-powered and connector-powered parts do not work for them; Docs open only for signed-in viewers; some artifacts show content only after sign-in.
- Source: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9547008-share-artifacts

### ChatGPT Sites (https://chatgpt.com/features/sites)
On Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise (not Free or Go). Share with selected people, the workspace, or publicly; invite teammates to edit and publish; rename the hosted URL or connect a custom domain; optional "Sign in with ChatGPT" and built-in storage. No password option mentioned.

## 3. Patterns

**Where SSO sits.**
- At the ~$20/seat "business" tier: Claude Team ($20 annual / $25 monthly, from 2 seats), ChatGPT Business ($20/$25, from 2 users), Notion Business ($20/$24).
- Higher: Gamma Business $40/seat; Figma Organization $55/full seat; Vercel charges $300/mo on top of Pro.
- Enterprise or sales-led only: Netlify, Framer, Webflow, Pitch, Loom; DocSend sells it as an add-on.
- Nobody among the small HTML-sharing tools (Tiiny, Stacktree, htmltourl, Static.app) offers SSO at all.

**Where audit logs sit.** Usually one step above SSO. Claude, ChatGPT, Notion and Gamma all put SSO on the business tier and audit logs on Enterprise. Exceptions: Netlify gives a 7-day audit log on Pro ($20 flat), and Figma bundles activity logs with SSO on Organization ($55).

**Viewer pricing.** Free viewers are the norm. Figma ("unlimited viewers… for free"), Framer ("viewers are free"), Vercel (unlimited free viewer seats), Netlify (unlimited free Reviewers), Webflow (free reviewer/commenter seats and comment-only links), Loom (free Creator Lite), DocSend (unlimited visitors) and Stacktree (no account) all follow it. Notion caps guests at 10 on Free only. Pitch caps *guest editors* by tier (2 / 5 / 25 / unlimited) but share links are free. Claude lets anyone open a public link but needs a Claude account for email invites. Many charge for a cheaper "light" seat: Figma Collab $3–5, Webflow Limited $15, Framer content editor $10–15.

**Per-seat price bands (annual / monthly).**
- Collaborative editor seat, no SSO: **$10–20** (Notion Plus $10/$12, Pitch Team $15–19/$18, Loom $15/$18, Figma Pro full seat $16/$20, Framer editor $20/$30). Webflow full seat ($39) and DocSend Standard ($45) sit above this band.
- Business tier with SSO: **$20–55** (Claude Team / ChatGPT Business / Notion Business $20–25, Gamma Business $40, Figma Org $55).
- Enterprise: custom, often with a minimum (Claude Enterprise: 20 seats).
- Annual discount: mostly 15–20% (Claude 15%, Notion 17–20%, Loom 17%, Pitch 17%, Figma 20%). Gamma (up to 28%), Framer (33%), DocSend (31–40%) and Webflow (up to 40%) go further.

**Flat team pricing is common in hosting.** Netlify Pro is $20 flat with unlimited members. Tiiny Pro is $31/$38 with the first extra member free. Static.app includes 3 seats on its $15 plan. Stacktree is flat per plan. Per-seat pricing dominates in the collaboration and AI tools.

**Seat minimums.** Claude Team and ChatGPT Business start at 2. DocSend Advanced includes 3 users. Claude Enterprise needs 20. None of the comparables uses a minimum of 5 for its SSO tier; Claude and ChatGPT sell SSO from 2 seats.

**Individual entry tier.** Paid entry clusters at **$8–13**: ChatGPT Go $8, Netlify Personal $9, htmltourl $9, Tiiny Tiny $9 monthly, Gamma Plus $9/$12, Notion Plus $10/$12, Framer Basic $10/$15, DocSend Personal $10/$15. Above that: Tiiny Solo $13/$18, Webflow Basic $15, Claude Pro $17/$20, ChatGPT Plus $20, Stacktree Solo $19. **A custom domain is usually not in the $9 tier.** It costs Tiiny Solo $13/$18, Gamma Pro $18/$25, Notion $10 plus an $8–10 add-on, Framer $10/$15 and Webflow $15. The exceptions are Netlify (Free) and Carrd ($19 per year).

Overall: the shape matches the market (free viewers, per-editor team pricing, SSO at the second paid tier). Every price sits at the bottom of its band. The cheapest change is to add a monthly-vs-annual split: about 20–25% more on monthly, with the current numbers as the annual price.
