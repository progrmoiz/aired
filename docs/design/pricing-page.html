Questions

Do the people I share with need to pay or sign up?
No. Viewers, commenters and guests are free on every plan. Invited people confirm their email once; they never create an aired account.
Team and Business aren't open yet. What happens if I join?
You're on the list and we email you when it opens. Nothing is charged until then.
What happens to my pages if I cancel Pro?
They stay up on aired.sh links, the badge comes back, and pages over the free limit stop being permanent.
Is there a discount for nonprofits or students?
Write to us and we'll sort something out.
