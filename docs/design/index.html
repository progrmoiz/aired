Refresh it: go light-first, keep the bones.

The dark developer look fits a CLI, not someone who just made a client report in ChatGPT. Recommendation: Direction B, "Paper". Light by default with dark mode kept for people who prefer it. Ink-black buttons like Claude, ChatGPT and Notion, purple kept for the logo mark and links, bigger reading text, the globe swapped for a picture of what the recipient sees. Ship the badge change first. It's the surface most people see, and it's a separate 15-line change.

What's wrong with the current style

Checked on the branch locally and live on aired.sh, at desktop and phone widths.

FindingEvidenceWhy it matters now
Wrong signal for the new audienceNear-black, purple glow, mono snippets, "TTL", "PIN", "Update token" and "Wire it up yourself" on the homepageThe new one-liner speaks to anyone who makes things with AI. The tools they use every day (Claude, ChatGPT, Notion, Google Docs) are all light, with a black primary button.
Helper text fails contrastTertiary #56565a on #0a0a0b is 2.7:1 (AA needs 4.5). It's used for the TTL/PIN labels, "⌘V to paste", captions and the footer.The labels a first-time visitor needs are the hardest to read.
Primary button fails contrastWhite on #7c6aef is 4.07:1 for 14px text. Disabled state at 30% opacity reads as muddy purple.The one button on the page.
14px body on long guidesThe three SEO guides are 600-line reading pages with comparison tables, set at 14px gray on black.Search visitors read these; the style was written for a 30-second tool page.
The globe costs more than it showsThree chained module requests (esm.sh → cobe → phenomenon), a 1000×1000 WebGL canvas redrawn every frame forever, shown at 35% opacity and barely visible. Hidden on phones, but the script still loads.LCP and battery on the pages meant to rank. Nothing about it explains the product.
Phone form clipsAt 375px the PIN placeholder shows as "op", and Upload loses its label.Most AI-chat sharing starts on a phone.
The badge ignores its pageA dark blurred pill with a purple mark on every page, light or dark. It says only "aired", links with no ?ref tag.Published pages have had 102,156 views (live /api/stats), each carrying the badge. On a white client report it reads as a foreign object, and a recipient can't tell what it is.

If you pick B, the order I'd ship it in

  1. Badge first, on its own. Pick a light or dark skin from the page background, say "Shared with aired", link to aired.sh/?ref=badge, and fix the injected OG description. It works with any direction and reaches the most people.
  2. Remove the globe. Put the "what they see" preview in its place. A perf win even before the restyle.
  3. Token swap. CSS variables, light default, dark under prefers-color-scheme. Shared form CSS moves into src/input.css.
  4. Type and copy. 16px reading text on guides and docs, a larger H1, "Expires" and "Password" labels. Optionally "Get link" instead of "Publish" on the homepage form.
  5. DESIGN.md. Rewrite §2 Color, §3 Typography and the audience line, then log it in §12.
