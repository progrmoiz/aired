aired.sh · design direction · round 2 · 23 Nov 2026

Pick a look that only aired could have.

Round 1 used the looks every AI-built page lands on. This round starts from what aired is: a name that means broadcast and airmail, and a job that is sending a page to someone. My pick is E, Airmail. It explains the product to a non-technical visitor at a glance, and it gives the badge a reason to exist: every letter carries a postmark. D, Signal is the runner-up if you'd rather lean on the name and the existing logo.

Three directions

Each has the hero with the publish form, pricing, and the badge on a light client report and a dark game. Open them full size; they link to each other.

Test-card colour bars as the only decoration, a red on-air lamp, the result shown as a TV lower third, pricing as a rate card. Wide Archivo headings like a station ident.

Effort
3–4 days
Risk
Colour bars can tip into retro if overused; keep them to the strip, the result and the badge.

E · Airmail Recommended

A link is something you send to a person.

The form is an airmail envelope with a stamp and postmark. Pricing is two postage stamps. The badge becomes a small postmark that says "Sent with aired". Pro removes it, like franking your own mail.

Effort
4–5 days
Risk
The metaphor has to stay light on guide and docs pages, which should read as plain text with the envelope only on the form.

F · Utility Safest

The page is the tool. No metaphor.

White, black and one signal blue. Square corners, thick rules, huge type. The drop target fills the width and the returned link is the biggest thing on screen. The badge is a blue square tab.

Effort
2–3 days
Risk
Confident but cold. Memorable for its type, not for an idea.

Same in every direction

  1. Badge first, on its own. Match the page background, say what it is, link to aired.sh/?ref=badge. It's on 102,156 page views so far, far more than the homepage.
  2. Drop the WebGL globe. Three chained requests and a canvas redrawn every frame, at 35% opacity.
  3. Fix contrast. Today's helper text is 2.7:1 and the button text 4.07:1; all three directions clear 4.5:1.
  4. Plain labels. "Expires" and "Password" instead of TTL and PIN.
