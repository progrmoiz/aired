aired.sh · design direction · 23 Nov 2026

Keep the look. Make it read like a product people pay for.

The dark style isn't why aired feels like a side project. Vercel, Linear and Raycast are dark and people pay for them. What reads as "free open-source tool" is what's on the page: GitHub in the main nav, GitHub-only sign-in, CLI tabs as the second section, a ticker of strangers' page titles, "Built by @handle", and no pricing anywhere. Fix those, keep the design.

Recommended: current look, product signals

Same palette, fonts, column and form as aired.sh today. Pricing, a Pro offer and a real footer added; developer material moved down.

What design can't fix

  1. There's nothing to buy yet. The pricing block only works with a real "Start Pro" checkout or waitlist behind it, which is step 5 of the growth plan.
  2. GitHub-only sign-in blocks the non-developers the copy now talks to. Google sign-in or an email link matters more than any color.
  3. The badge is what most people see (102,156 page views so far). It should say "Shared with aired" and match the page under it, as in the mockup.
