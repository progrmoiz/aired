Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need an account?
No. Paste or drop the file and you get a link. Sign in only if you want your pages in one place.
Does the person I send it to need anything?
Just a browser. No account, no app, no sign-in.
How long does a link last?
Seven days by default. Pick 1 hour, 1 day, 30 days, or keep it permanently with an account.
Can I keep a page private?
Add a password before you publish. Pages without an account are hidden from search engines.
What can I publish?
Any single HTML page up to 2 MB: reports, dashboards, decks, games. Scripts and styles run as written.
What happens if I cancel Pro?
Your pages stay up on aired.sh links and the badge comes back.
